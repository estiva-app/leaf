# PartView

One block of another page or issue, live under a comment or under the box
(SPEC §13.6.1), in the kit's **PartCard** frame: what it is part of, then the
block as it is now.

**Seen in**: *Comments/PartView*: **Of A Document**, **Long**, **Of A Ship
Issue**, **Loading**, **Not Available**, **Deleted**, **Block Deleted**,
**Cannot Be Shown**; under a sent comment and under the comment box.

## When

- A block link in a comment: the part the comment carries (`part`).

## When not

- A whole file named in a comment → **CommentCard**, its card.
- A block of the page the comment is on: it draws nothing, its chip is enough.

## How

```tsx
import { PartView } from './PartView'

<PartView pointer={{ address, block }} />
```

`PartFrame` draws a read already made, for a story.

## What it owns

- Reading the part, again every 30 s while the tab is visible. A read that
  comes back empty asks whether a trusted deletion names the file (protocol's
  `readAbsence`): "deleted" then, else "not available" (SPEC §13.6.1).
- Which of the SPEC's five states it is in, drawn by the kit's **PartCard**
  states in their words; one that cannot be shown links to the whole file.
- Where its title opens, at the block: a Leaf document here, in this tab;
  another app's file in that app, in a new tab, at the block when the app
  declares a link to one. There is no "Open" button: it said again what the
  title does (Katerina, 31 Oct).
- A long block cut behind Show more at 160px, as Peek and Ship cut theirs.
- A document labelled "Document", by its type.
