# SummaryCount

The count a summary past about 240 characters shows under it: a warning,
never a refusal.

**Seen in**: *Document/SummaryCount*: **Long Summary**; and under the summary
on a page, while it is being written.

## When

- Under a page's summary while it is being written: **PageSummary** draws it
  under the line on the page, and New document under its Summary field.

## When not

- To stop someone typing: a summary is never cut or refused (Miky, 15 Oct).
- For any other line under a field → **FieldLine**, or **Field**'s `helper`.

## How

```tsx
import { SummaryCount } from '../document/SummaryCount'

<SummaryCount text={draft} />
```

## What it owns

- **The limit**, 240, and **counting as a person does**: what would be saved,
  letter by letter. Nothing at or under the limit.
