# TeamChip

The team a page's thing belongs to: the one chip right after its name in the
page's bar, the package's **Chip** with the team icon at a chip's 12px, as Peek
draws it on an initiative's page and on another app's file.

<Canvas of={Stories.InAnInitiativesBar} />

## When

- In **PageHeader**'s `after`, on an initiative's page and on a document's
  page, when the team is known. On another app's file it comes first, then
  `OpenIn`.

## When not

- Anywhere but a page's bar: a list row says its team in its own column
  (**ListTable**).
- Beside another chip: a page's bar holds one chip, its team (Katerina, 17
  November). An initiative that lists the file is not a chip here.

## How

```tsx
import { TeamChip } from './TeamChip'

<PageHeader title={title} after={team ? <TeamChip name={team} /> : undefined} />
```

- Leaf has no team page, so the chip names the team and opens nothing.

## What it owns

Only the icon and that it is the team. The look is **Chip**'s.
