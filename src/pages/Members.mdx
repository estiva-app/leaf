# Members

Who is in a document or an initiative: the package's **MembersPill** in the
page's bar, and the **MembersDialog** it opens, with Join or Leave for you and
Add members once you are in, as Peek's and Ship's pages have them.

<Canvas of={Stories.Open} />

## When

- In **PageHeader**'s `members`, on a document's page and on an initiative's
  page.
- With `roles` on an initiative's page: its author's row says "Author", and an
  author who left is listed after the rest, "· not following", as Ship's.

## When not

- In a list's row, which opens its thing: the package's **MembersPill** with
  `readOnly`, nothing to press (**ListTable**'s Members column).
- A team's people: Leaf has no team page.

## How

```tsx
import { Members } from './Members'

<PageHeader title={title} members={<Members members={listed} me={me} directory={directory} roles={roles} onSetMine={setMine} onAdd={add} />} />
```

- `members` is `undefined` until read: nothing is drawn rather than a wrong
  count. A failed read stays `undefined`.
- `readOnly` on an archived file or initiative: no Join, Leave or Add.

## What it owns

Who is listed in which order (you first, then by name), each row's caption,
and which of Join, Leave and Add it offers. The pill's and the dialog's look
are the package's.
