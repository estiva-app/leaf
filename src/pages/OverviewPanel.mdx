# OverviewPanel

A page's Overview: the kit's **Panel** "Overview" in the column beside the
page, in the comments' place, empty for now (ONE-5, step 5; Katerina,
3 November).

**Seen in**: *Pages/OverviewPanel*: **Empty**.

## When

- Beside a page, when the header's side-panel button ("Show side panel") is
  pressed. It takes the comments' place, and hiding it brings back what the
  column held before (`useSideColumn`).

## When not

- Not for the comments. They are the column's other occupant,
  **CommentsColumn**, the kit's one column.
- Not for the page's Topics. They stay under the title (`FileTopicsOf`).

## How

```tsx
import { OverviewPanel } from './OverviewPanel'

{overview.open && (
  <>
    <Divider orientation="vertical" />
    <div className="flex flex-none flex-col" data-overview-column>
      <OverviewPanel />
    </div>
  </>
)}
```

## What it owns

- **Its name**, "Overview", in the panel's bar.
- **Its empty line**, "Nothing here yet.", the kit's **EmptyState** for one
  part of a page, placed by the kit's Panel padding (16px in, 12px under the
  bar). Nothing is planned for it yet.
- **Its width**: 384px, the comments column's, so the page does not move
  when one replaces the other.
